How it works

If you make games

  1. Post your game: a link, a screenshot, how many players it wants.
  2. People play it with their friends and vote.
  3. If it's good it goes up. If it's bad in a fun way it also goes up sometimes.

If you play games

  1. Find something weird on the front page.
  2. Find three more people on the crew board.
  3. Play it. Vote. Tell the dev what broke.

FAQ

Is this a joke?

The site looks like one. The games are real and so are the devs. Post yours.

Do I need a Steam page?

No. An itch.io link, a Steam page, a Google Drive zip, a build your friend sent you at 2am. We'll look at the zip before it goes up, because we're not monsters.

What does "go viral" actually mean here?

Top-voted games get the front page and we push them everywhere we can reach: socials, the newsletter, the Discord, our own group chats. It's a small machine right now. It gets bigger with you in it.

I'm a player, not a dev. Why am I here?

Because friendslop needs friends. Browse games, grab a crew, play, vote. The devs literally cannot do the last three for you.

Can I post someone else's game?

Yes, if you credit them and they're cool with it. Posting your friend's game is kind of the whole point.
